# Community Voting Blurb

**OpenGap — Find what's missing in research, and show the evidence.**

OpenGap helps a researcher test a research idea using OpenAIRE publications, projects, software and datasets. Instead of returning another list of papers or an opaque AI score, it shows one potential gap, explains exactly why the heuristic fired, and lets the user inspect the underlying OpenAIRE evidence. The goal is simple: turn a vague idea into an evidence-backed hypothesis worth investigating further.
