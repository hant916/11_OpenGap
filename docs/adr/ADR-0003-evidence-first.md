# ADR-0003 — Evidence before intelligence theater

## Status
Accepted.

## Decision
Every primary finding must expose the measured values and representative OpenAIRE records that caused it.

## Rejected
- opaque AI confidence scores;
- “opportunity” scores;
- unsupported narrative conclusions;
- generated citations not returned by the provider.

## Consequence
The UI may look simpler, but it is more defensible and better aligned with open-science reproducibility/transparency.
